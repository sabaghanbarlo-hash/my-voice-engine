Dear Diary ♡

I genuinely cannot believe this is my life now. Sometimes I just sit back and think about how far I've come. I actually built the life I used to dream about.

I'm financially free, a millionaire, and a successful businesswoman with multiple businesses and income streams. My language-learning brand has grown so much! Our Instagram and YouTube communities are huge, and my other YouTube channel has become incredibly successful too. People genuinely love what I create.

I have my dream house in the most beautiful neighborhood in the city. My office is everything I ever wanted: my computer, PS5, recording equipment, and all my creative setups. My black-and-red BMW is sitting in the garage next to my beautiful Mercedes-Benz collection. I still get excited every time I drive them!

My coffee station is my favorite part of the morning. Fresh beans, my dream espresso machine, and everything I need to make café-quality coffee. I also have closets full of clothes, makeup, and cosplay outfits. I can finally enjoy shopping without worrying about prices.

I love my body, my appearance, and how confident I've become. I feel beautiful, feminine, healthy, and strong. I play my guitars, sing, create videos, cosplay, and enjoy all my hobbies without feeling like I don't have enough time.

My parents are retired and financially free. I bought them a beautiful home, and seeing them enjoy such a peaceful life makes me happier than almost anything else.

I've traveled to Paris in autumn, Tokyo in spring, and New York during Christmas. I've seen so many places I once only dreamed about.

And my love life? Absolutely beautiful. I have the most wonderful, gentlemanly partner. He's affectionate, devoted, intelligent, and everything I wanted. We're so happy together.

The best part is that I don't feel trapped anymore. I have freedom, opportunities, experiences, and so much love in my life.

I can work when I want, travel when I want, buy what I love, spend time with my family, and enjoy being myself.

Sometimes I remember the girl who used to write about these dreams and wonder how she would react if she could see me now.

I hope she's proud.

Because I really, really love my life.

I made it. And there's still so much more to experience. ♡
